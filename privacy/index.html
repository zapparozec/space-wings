Space Wings Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Space Wings is a single-player game for the Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

No data is collected

Space Wings does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game makes no network connections: it has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or online features, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your Mac

Your campaign progress and settings (controls, audio, graphics and gameplay options) are saved only on your Mac, inside the app's own storage. They never leave your computer. Deleting the app removes them.

Speech

Radio voices are produced on your Mac by the speech voices built into macOS. No text or audio is sent anywhere.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
