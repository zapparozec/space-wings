Carrier fighter space combat

Fly with the Ravens squadron off the carrier Meridian: 24 story missions, three endings and choices that change who flies beside you.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

The Kraith are coming, and the carrier Meridian is the last line between them and home.

Space Wings is a story-driven space combat game for the Mac. You are Alex "Sparrow" Ward, the youngest pilot of the Ravens squadron, launching from the flight deck of the TCS Meridian into the Halcyon Reach. Dogfight alien interceptors, escort convoys and bombers, tear down capital ships and fly into the jaws of the Maw.

A CAMPAIGN WITH CONSEQUENCES

  • 24 story missions across three acts, with branches and three different endings
  • Your choices matter: spare or kill a rival ace, obey or defy an order, and live with what follows
  • Squadmates with their own voices and stories: Anvil, Duchess, Tex, Mule, Ghost and more
  • In-engine cutscenes before and after every mission, with portraits of everyone who speaks
  • Replay any mission you have reached, and watch any cutscene from the main menu

FLY IT YOUR WAY

  • Cockpit and chase views, afterburner, glide mode, missiles and decoys
  • Give orders to your wingmen: attack my target, form up, break, cover me
  • Tactical map, autopilot to the next nav point and target cycling
  • Mouse and keyboard with fully remappable controls, or a game controller
  • Five difficulty levels, from relaxed to merciless

A LIVING BATTLEFIELD

  • Carrier launches and landings, bomber torpedo runs and capital ship broadsides
  • Asteroid fields, nebulae, ice rings and gas giants
  • Voiced radio chatter from your wing, the carrier and the enemy
  • Built for Apple silicon with Metal

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts. Space Wings works fully offline and collects no data.
