Questions, bug reports or ideas: write to retroweapon1@gmail.com. Please include your Mac model and macOS version.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Controls

SteerMouse (captured while flying) or arrow keys; left stick on a controller
RollA / D or Q / E
ThrottleW / S; X stops, Z matches the target's speed
Afterburner / glideTab / Shift
Guns / missileSpace or left click / F or right click
DecoysC
TargetsT next target, R whoever is attacking you
View, autopilot, mapV cockpit or chase view, N autopilot, M tactical map
Wingman orders1 attack my target, 2 form up, 3 break, 4 cover me
PauseEsc

All flight controls can be changed in Settings → Controls.

Common questions

How do I start over? Choose NEW CAMPAIGN in the main menu. Missions you have reached stay available in MISSION SELECT.

The radio voices sound robotic. Better voices can be downloaded in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices.

Can I watch the story again? Yes: CUTSCENES in the main menu plays any cutscene.
